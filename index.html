About Me

Creating content is something I have always enjoyed. I was first introduced into the digital media world when I was very young, and owe most of my inspiration, drive, and creativity to my loving mother. I would sneak onto her computer when she wasn't using it for work and mess around with good ole' Microsoft Paint. This is when I discovered the beautiful world of digital art. I soon found myself drawing, coloring, crafting, collecting, and grabbing any other office supplies to further feul my artistic ambitions.

Soon, I was introduced to websites like MySpace and Tumblr early on, which started to fuel my desire to customize websites and design beautiful things for the web.

Shortly after graduating high school, I joined the team at Red Palm Marketing, and found myself doing all of things that I loved while learning many new skills. Web design, graphic design, video editing, SEO, marketing, and much more.

I decided to go all in with my creativity and enrolled at St. Petersburg College in Florida to further explore my creative side. I focused primarily on video production and learned the tips and tricks of becoming a proficient video editor, writer, and producer. I graduated with my AS in Video Production, and have since then continued to further my education in the digital media field through various work opportunities.

All in all, I've always enjoyed the digital media world and creating things from my computer at any time and anywhere! I plan to keep learning, creating, and sharing everything I do with the world!

This is my passion, this is what I love to do.
